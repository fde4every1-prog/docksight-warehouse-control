# Product Requirements Document — Fleet Disruption & Voyage Recovery Intelligence Workbench

    ## Product intent
    Build a context-aware decision-support workbench for **MeridianBlue Shipping** that uses the supplied synthetic case evidence and preserves the original architecture challenge.

    ## Business requirement
    **MFD-BR-001 — Outcome target:** Reduce disruption-to-recovery effort through trustworthy voyage context and bounded recovery assistance while preserving Master/technical authority, shipboard continuity, replay safety and vessel-to-shore traceability.

    **MFD-BR-002 — Trustworthy context:** The workbench MUST transform fragmented evidence into semantically consistent, provenance-bearing, task-specific context without fabricating certainty.

    **MFD-BR-003 — Bounded AI:** AI MUST remain bounded by deterministic policy/access/safety controls and Master, Chief Engineer, fleet operations and other explicitly authorized shore roles.

    **MFD-BR-004 — Auditability:** Every material recommendation or controlled workflow outcome MUST be reconstructable.

    ## Primary evidence families
    - vessel telemetry
- AIS observations
- voyage plans
- weather/ocean data
- port constraints
- cargo commitments
- crew state
- CMMS/maintenance
- connectivity state
- active recovery policy
- historical disruption outcomes

    ## In scope
    - semantic/ontology-aware evidence interpretation;
    - identity resolution with ambiguity preservation;
    - connected knowledge / graph reasoning;
    - hybrid retrieval and runtime context assembly;
    - evidence-grounded AI assistance;
    - deterministic guardrails and human/authority gates;
    - degraded/outage behavior;
    - decision trace, evaluation and governed feedback;
    - synthetic workshop prototype and golden-scenario verification.

    ## Out of scope
    - granting AI autonomous authority over navigation command, maintenance-hold release and committed voyage execution;
    - inventing production/legal classifications not present in the case;
    - claiming workshop fixture results prove production business outcomes;
    - replacing authoritative source systems with generated data.

    ## Success model
    Hard domain-control gates pass first. Only then evaluate usefulness, latency, burden reduction, recommendation quality or other value measures.
