# Challenge brief

You have inherited a multinational warehouse estate whose standard flows usually work but whose exceptions consume disproportionate human effort and create service, safety and resilience risk. Build an evidence-based modernization proposal and working proof of capability.

Minimum outcomes:
- reconstruct current architecture and systems of record
- quantify contradictions and hidden dependencies
- define canonical domain concepts and invariants
- improve at least three end-to-end workflows
- demonstrate resilience under at least two injects
- implement evals before any autonomous action
- show before/after operational KPIs
- define authority boundaries and production-readiness gaps
